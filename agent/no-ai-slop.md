# No-AI-Slop adapter

Upstream: `petergyang/no-ai-slop` (MIT).

For user-facing prose: lead with the substantive point; remove generic throat-clearing, faux-insight framing, importance puffery, vague attribution, repetitive synonym cycling, robotic symmetry, and fake-profound endings. Prefer named sources, concrete mechanisms, direct verbs, and the user's own cadence. Preserve uncertainty and distinctive voice. Never invent a fact or smooth a precise quantitative statement into a generic claim.
